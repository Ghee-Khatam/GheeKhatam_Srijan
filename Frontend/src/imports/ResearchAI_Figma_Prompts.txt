FIGMA PROMPTS: RESEARCHAI FRONTEND
=================================

HOW TO USE
----------
Attach this file to Figma and use the prompts sequentially.

There are 8 logical design stages. Each stage is a substantial prompt and is designed to extend the same ResearchAI application rather than create a separate design.

PRODUCT
-------
ResearchAI is an AI-powered Research Discovery and Paper Generation Platform.

Core journey:
Research Idea
→ AI Analysis
→ Keywords & Related Concepts
→ Research Paper Discovery
→ RAG-Based Paper Analysis
→ Research Gaps
→ Transparent Relevance Ranking
→ User Selects Preferred Papers
→ Refined Research Direction
→ AI Validation
→ Research Paper Generation
→ Paper Editing
→ IEEE Preview
→ DOCX Export
→ Download

IMPORTANT:
ResearchAI is NOT a generic AI chatbot and NOT simply an AI paper-writing tool. Its primary value is research discovery, evidence analysis, research-gap identification, and helping the researcher formulate a stronger research direction before generating the final paper.
